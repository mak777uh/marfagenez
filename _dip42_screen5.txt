We hypothesize that the PCC is involved in the generation of the PKD-ELV and is a structural component thereof. Here, we investigated whether the presence of a variety of biomolecules affects the overall growth of vesicles composed of decanoic acid, a prebiotically plausible fatty acid, upon micelle addition. We find that the initial radius and final radius of vesicles are largely unaffected by the presence of the additional compounds.
